# Glossary

## Actionable

A contribution is actionable when a maintainer can understand what changed,
why it matters, and how to verify it.

## Evidence

Evidence is anything that supports the contribution: a failing test, a
reproduction command, a screenshot, a linked issue, or a before/after example.

## Impact

Impact explains who benefits from the contribution and what gets better.

## Risk

Risk names what could break, become stricter, or need maintainer attention after
the change.

## Verification

Verification is the command or manual check used to confirm the contribution
does what it claims.
